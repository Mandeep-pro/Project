Tourists in Sikkim face connectivity gaps in hilly terrain, safety risks from unpredictable weather, and disconnect from authentic local culture. Meanwhile, priceless cultural heritage needs a modern platform for preservation and accessibility.

A Complete Ecosystem

Emergency SOS

Feel secure with our low-latency, one-tap SOS framework. Encrypted alerts send directly to SSDMA authorities for immediate response.
